## What is does.software

[does.software](https://does.software/?ref=github) gives out free subdomains under `does.software` and serves them for you. You pick a word, and that name can:

- Redirect visitors to any URL (your site, GitHub, LinkedIn, a Notion page…) over HTTPS. There are no certificates to manage.
- Forward mail sent to `something@yourname.does.software` to your Gmail, Outlook or any other inbox.
- Do both at once. The redirect and the forwarding live on the same name, and you can change either one whenever you want.
- Send specific paths to their own destination, so `yourname.does.software/cv` opens your CV and `yourname.does.software/blog` your blog.

Each name also counts its clicks and forwarded emails, without cookies or visitor profiles.

## How to get a free subdomain

1. Go to [does.software/request](https://does.software/request/?ref=github) and type the word you want. The form checks as you type whether it's free.
2. Claim it. With an account, the name goes live the moment you submit the form; without one, as soon as you confirm your email. You can also sign in with Google, GitHub or GitLab.
3. Tell it where to go, in the same form or later from your panel:
   - Redirect visitors to: the URL that `yourname.does.software` should open.
   - Forward mail to: the inbox that gets what's sent to your name. That inbox receives a confirmation email first, and the forward starts once you accept it.

   Fill in at least one of the two, or both.
4. Open `https://yourname.does.software` and check that it takes you where you said.

Sensitive names (big brands, words like `login` or `billing`, institutional terms) go through a short manual review first, usually answered in less than a day. If the name you want is taken, you can join its waitlist and you'll get an email if it frees up.

## Questions

### Is it really free?

Yes. The free plan has no card and no end date: one name, 1 email forward and 2 path redirects, renewed with one click every 45 days.

### Do I need to set up DNS?

No. does.software serves the redirect, the forwarding and the HTTPS certificate. You fill in a form and the name works.

### Is it the same as is-a.dev or is-a.software?

No, they are separate projects with similar names. With does.software you set up a redirect and email without touching DNS. is-a.dev and is-a.software give you DNS records to point at a host of your own. [Comparison](https://does.software/guides/does-software-vs-is-a/?ref=github).

### Can I use it with GitHub Pages?

You can point the redirect at your `github.io` page on any plan. Hosting the site on the name itself, including publishing it from a GitHub or GitLab repo, comes with the trial and the paid plans. [Guide](https://does.software/guides/publish-from-github/?ref=github).

### Is it a Freenom alternative?

If what you used a free Freenom domain for was a redirect or an email address, yes. [Alternatives for a free domain](https://does.software/guides/freenom-alternatives/?ref=github).
